# Privacy

Stash does not collect, store elsewhere or send any personal data about you.

- **No accounts.** The app never asks you to sign in.
- **No analytics, no tracking, no advertising.**
- **No network connections.** Stash contains no networking code: it doesn't check for updates, doesn't phone home and doesn't upload anything. Text recognition in images runs on your Mac with Apple's Vision framework.
- **Everything stays on your Mac.** Your clipboard history and settings live in `~/Library/Application Support/Stash/`. Entries are deleted automatically when they expire (1, 3 or 7 days; pinned items stay until you remove them), and you can delete any item at any time.
- **What it can see.** To do its job Stash reads whatever you copy to the clipboard, including text you might consider sensitive, such as a password you copied from a password manager. There is no filter for this in the current version, so shorter retention and deleting an item with ✕ are your tools.

Questions about privacy: open an [issue](../../issues).
